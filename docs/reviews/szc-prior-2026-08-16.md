# The sum-to-zero prior: measured, tested, reverted, and now a decision for Pete

2026-08-16. Immutable record. The change is **not in the code** — it was
reverted per a pre-registration written before the run.

## What the prior is

`szc_sd_pts` pins the level of the house effects. They are identified only up
to a constant — lift every pollster a point and drop the latent trend a point
and the fit is identical — so something must hold the level, and this does. It
encodes **how far the polling industry as a whole may sit from the truth**.

It was hand-set at 0.3 points and never measured. Until today it was not even
reachable from the public API: it lived as a default on an internal function,
so it could not be varied, tested or estimated.

## What the record says it should be

Consensus of the final polls at every completed election since 1990, against
the result. `OTH` excluded — its −5.3 average miss is the known
fold-into-Others parsing artefact, not pollster bias.

| Window | n party-elections | sd of miss |
|---|---:|---:|
| final 14 days | 147 | **1.61** |
| final 30 days | 187 | 1.94 |
| final 60 days | 209 | 2.14 |

The spread shrinks as the window narrows, so the wider figures partly measure
opinion moving rather than pollsters erring. **About 1.5 points**, against a
prior of 0.3 — four to five times too tight. Mean miss +0.09: the field is not
biased in a direction, it misses *together*, in a direction that varies.

## What was pre-registered, and what happened

Four checks, written down before the pipeline ran:

| | Requirement | Result | |
|---|---|---|---|
| **SZ1** | Two-party moves < 0.5 points | 47.79 → 48.00, **+0.21** | PASS |
| **SZ2** | Max house effect must *increase*, stay < 8 | 4.13 → **3.98** (decreased) | **FAIL** |
| **SZ3** | L4b herding floor still holds | 1.00, unchanged | PASS |
| **SZ4** | All validation endpoints inside pre-registered bounds | V2, V5, A1–A4, N1–N3, P1–P4, B2/B3 all pass | PASS |

**Reverted on SZ2, as committed in the pre-registration.**

## Why SZ2 failed, and why that is my error not the model's

SZ2 required individual house effects to grow, reasoning that otherwise the
change did nothing. That was wrong about the mechanism. `szc` constrains the
**weighted mean** of house effects, not their individual size — which is
governed by `sigma_house_pts` and the data.

Measured directly, the prior does exactly what it should:

| `szc_sd_pts` | max weighted-mean house effect | max individual |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.1 | 0.13 | 3.27 |
| 0.3 (current) | 0.26 | 3.24 |
| 1.0 | 1.48 | 4.13 |
| 1.5 | 2.21 | 4.85 |
| 3.0 | 3.17 | 5.80 |

The quantity `szc` governs rises cleanly. With the mean free to drift,
individual effects re-centre, and in the full pipeline — which also has firm
noise factors, the fold correction and estimated per-cycle sigmas — the
largest ended up *closer* to the new mean.

Second error worth recording: **the sensitivity sweep predicted the wrong
sign.** It ran `fit_cycle_trends` bare and showed max individual effects
rising to 4.85 at 1.5; the full pipeline gave 3.98. A stripped-down harness is
not the model.

## The result that was not pre-registered

Held-out mean absolute error, 195 election-horizon pairs, the figure that
decides whether the forecast is actually better:

| | held-out MAE |
|---|---:|
| `szc_sd_pts` = 0.3 | 2.0850 |
| `szc_sd_pts` = 1.5 | **2.0588** |

**1.3% better out of sample**, with interval coverage (B2/B3) still passing.

## Where that leaves it — Pete's call

Every criterion that tested whether the model is *better* or still *valid*
passed. The only failure was my own guess about the internal mechanism, and
that guess is now measurably wrong.

But held-out MAE was not the pre-registered criterion, and adopting it *after*
seeing it favour the change is precisely the move pre-registration exists to
prevent. I am not going to re-register my way to an answer I already know.

Three options:

1. **Adopt 1.5.** Justified by a measured prior (1.5 against 0.3), a 1.3%
   out-of-sample improvement, and every validity check passing. Cost: the
   deciding criterion was chosen after the fact.
2. **Re-run properly.** Fresh pre-registration naming held-out MAE as the
   primary criterion, on a value chosen from the measurement alone — then
   accept the result whatever it is. Slower, clean.
3. **Leave at 0.3** and record this as a negative result on the pre-registered
   terms.

Recommendation: **(2)**. The evidence for the change is good and the process
for adopting it is not, and on a number that gets published the process is
worth the extra run. (1) would be defensible if stated as openly as this
document does, but it sets the precedent that a failed check can be replaced
by a passing one after the fact, which is the habit that costs the most later.
