### 1. Why the departed-hold failed, and what could be adjusted

Cause of the original problem (verified): the 0.38 departed-leader retention
(`departed_rate = c(IND = 0.38)`, `R/dev_slope.R`) was measured on FINAL shares
(`docs/reviews/departed-leader-retention-2026-09-15.md:30`) but applied as a slope BEFORE the seat
row is renormalised to 100. The row then sums to ~59-69, renormalisation scales every cell back up,
and the decayed independent ends at an effective 0.6-0.7. New England 2013: prior 52.0 -> 21.1 after
slope -> 35.9 after renormalise (actual 20.4). Lyne 2013: 39.8 -> 16.5 -> 24.0 (actual 7.6).

The fix (hold the decayed cell, scale only the others) is built, off by default, byte-identical
off. It was refused by its own pre-registration (`docs/plans/prereg-departed-hold-fixed-2026-10-04.md`),
16 pairs, five harnesses, `AUSPOL_XGB_PRIMARY=0`, 500 sims (`docs/reviews/departed-hold-sweep-2026-10-05.md`):

- Arm A (hold every class where the decay fires, 2,872 cells): primary passes (-0.053 points,
  2.1 SE) but actual-weighted share RMSE +0.020 (1.4 SE worse; limit is 1 SE) and the
  independent-win-probability disqualifier fires. 96% of held cells are classes under 15% prior
  share, which the 0.38 was never measured on; the damage is Kennedy fed2013 OTH_RIGHT (25.8 -> 18.9,
  actual 38.9; 8,882 of +15,050 weighted error), Mount Gambier sa2026, Warringah fed2019 etc.
- Arm B (post hoc, Amendment 1: only classes with prior share >= 15, the measured population,
  86 cells): RMSE and log loss fine, but primary is -0.79, SE 0.97 (0.8 SE, only 14 pairs, 0-28
  cells per pair) and IND win probability still falls in two seats: Mount Gambier sa2026
  0.49 -> 0.22 and Kavel sa2026 0.96 -> 0.87.
- Successors to retiring sitting independents kept 0.16 (Lyne), 0.33 (New England), 0.43 (Kavel),
  0.59 (Mount Gambier) of the old vote. The 0.38 average is right; the spread is wide and the model
  sees nothing that separates them.

Ideas for adjusting it (NONE tested; design them with Pete on rows before writing, per CLAUDE.md):

a. **Widen the uncertainty, not just move the mean.** The win-probability disqualifier fires because
   the held mean drops but the spread stays narrow, while the real spread of successor retention is
   about 0.16-0.59. Give held cells a standard deviation fitted from the 305-case dispersion
   (`fit_xgb_primary_sd.R` already wants wider SDs for independents). Likely addresses the
   win-probability and log-loss side directly.
b. **Pair the hold with a signal that separates a strong successor from a weak one** (item 2 below):
   an endorsement, local salience, a council-level record. Retention = shrunk 0.38 + signal. The
   rule cannot see an endorsed successor today (Indi 2019 Haines, McGowan's pick, kept 0.93).
c. **More cases for the primary clause.** WA is not wired (no `screened_slopes()` call in
   `backtest_candidate_wa.R`, a pre-existing gap); wiring it adds 7 pairs. Cluster the primary on
   seat rather than pair only if a new prereg says so, not as a rescue.
d. **Any new arm needs a new, visible prereg amendment**, the retrained-xgb arm (4-step retrain,
   `docs/reviews/xgb-primary-circularity-2026-09-13.md`) and the 20,000-sim deciding run. The
   base_pred AND xgb layers must both be tested (CLAUDE.md "primary-vote fix must be tested in
   base_pred AND the xgb layer").

Tools already built: `renorm_hold()` and `screened_slopes(with_flags=TRUE)` in `R/dev_slope.R`;
switches `AUSPOL_DEPARTED_HOLD`, `AUSPOL_DEPARTED_HOLD_MIN_PRIOR`, `AUSPOL_DEPARTED_HOLD_DUMP=<csv>`
(writes the held cells); `scripts/run_departed_hold_sweep.sh`, `..._armB.sh` and
`scripts/score_departed_hold_sweep.R` (the driver hardcodes this session's scratchpad path in `S=`;
edit it, and export each harness's HEAD script to `$S/sweep/base_<h>.R` with `git show`).

## Pitfalls hit this session (do not repeat)

- **`err_xgb`, `err_base` in `output/forecasts.csv` are ABSOLUTE errors.** I read them as signed and
  told Pete "all 100 worst misses are under-calls". The truth is 75 under, 25 over. Signed =
  `actual_share - xgb_pred`.
- `output/candidacies.csv` `surname` is EMPTY for nsw, qld, vic and most sa rows; match on `name`.
  A surname match gave three false "departed" cases (Mount Gambier, Frome, Florey).
- `Rscript` heredocs through the Bash tool lost a backslash (`"\\.md$"` became an error); use a
  fixed string pattern or the Edit tool. A stray `python3 - <<EOF` hung the shell until the memory
  watchdog stopped it. Do not call `python3` here.
- Do NOT edit `scripts/` or `R/` while a background sweep runs (Rscript re-reads the file).
- Hand-run harnesses write into `output/`; the sweep driver moves new files out afterwards. Check
  `git status --short output` is empty after any hand run.
- The bottleneck hook counts a long `sleep` wait as run time; it is not compute.
- Free memory was 2 to 4 GB; one harness run is about 30 s per pair and fine.
