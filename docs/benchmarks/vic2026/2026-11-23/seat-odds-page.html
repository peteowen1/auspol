Edge is the expected return on each dollar if the chosen source is right: its win chance times the decimal price, minus 1. Kelly share is the fraction of bankroll the Kelly rule stakes on that one bet, edge divided by (price minus 1), scaled by the Stake setting; it is zero when the edge is not positive. "Bookie implies" is one divided by the price, margin included. Prices are each bookmaker's at 9:16am Tuesday 24 November for its four shortest outcomes per district; "Any other" and independent prices are left out, as is Ripon's Coalition price (Liberal and Nationals priced separately against one Coalition chance).

Our model's Labor chances in Box Hill and Ashwood come from a known weak spot (how it fills in One Nation's vote there), so read those two edges with that in mind.

These Kelly stakes treat every bet as if it were the only one. Seats move together on the night, so a full slate staked this way is far larger, and riskier, than any single line suggests; the total shown is the plain sum.
