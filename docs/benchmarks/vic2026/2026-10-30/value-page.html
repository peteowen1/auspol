Is the Victoria model ready, and where are the bookies wrong?

Usable now, not finished.

The forecast publishes daily, CI is green and it beats AE Forecasts on the seven-election track record. But candidate nominations do not close until 9 November, several screens are queued but not run, and three known defects sit exactly in the seats where we disagree most with the bookies. Treat the value list below as a shortlist to check, not a list to bet.

Which odds look like value

Click a seat to see both sides' numbers for every candidate, every book's price, the 2022 result, and for the contested seats what would settle who is right. The review behind those notes is docs/reviews/contested-seats-vic2026-2026-10-28.md.

Edge is what $1 returns on average if our probability is right: our probability × best price − 1. +0.50 means 50c profit per dollar. Every price is the best available across the three books for that wording of the outcome (books word some outcomes differently, such as "Other" and "Any Other", so those appear as separate rows). The grade says whether we know of a reason our number is wrong there.

Clean no known problem Contested plausible reason the bookies are closer Known defect our number is suspect; skip
SeatBackBest priceBookBookies' probOur probEdge¼ Kelly stakeGradeWhy

Bookies' prob is the margin-removed probability (power method), averaged over the books pricing that seat. ¼ Kelly stake is the share of a betting bank, a quarter of the full Kelly amount, which is still aggressive for a model that has not seen a 20% One Nation vote. Ripon is left out: Sportsbet and TAB price Liberal and Nationals separately there and we forecast them as one Coalition class. A named independent is matched to our whole independent class.
