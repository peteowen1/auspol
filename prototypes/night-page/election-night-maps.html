Election night maps

Victoria, 2022 state election replayed booth by booth. Drag the slider or press Play to watch the count arrive. Tap a district to open its booths. Boundaries are the official 2022 districts (ABS 2022 state electoral divisions).

Who leads each district

Colour is the model's most likely winner; fainter means less certain. Arrows show the swing in the Labor-minus-Coalition first-preference gap, 2018 to 2022: left is toward Labor, right toward the Coalition. District arrows fade in as the count proceeds; they use the full 2022 district totals (prototype simplification).

Booths in

Tap a booth for its numbers.
Dot size is the booth's total ordinary votes. Smaller booths are counted first in this replay (a stand-in for the real order); the district's counted share comes from the model replay. A booth arrow appears once the booth is counted and matches a 2018 booth of the same name in the same district ().
Notes (unconfirmed items marked). Boundaries are the ABS 2022 state electoral divisions (the 2022 districts, which the 2026 election also uses), simplified for the web. Narracan has no booths in the booth file, so its booth map is empty. Swing is on first preferences: the Labor share minus the Coalition share, 2022 less 2018, where all other votes stay in the denominator. A district or booth with no 2018 counterpart shows no arrow.
